A DDJ-400 or DDJ-FLX4 over Web MIDI (Chrome or Edge). PLAY, the pads in HOT CUE (steps), BEAT JUMP (tracks, mutes) and SAMPLER (patterns), LOAD, the faders, crossfader and TEMPO, the jogs' sides (tracks), right CUE/LOOP CALL (step page; SHIFT: a bar), BEAT FX. Mouse: click steps, tracks, patterns. Keys: space, arrows.
