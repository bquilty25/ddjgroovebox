Browsers start audio after a click. A DDJ-400 or DDJ-FLX4 plays it over Web MIDI: allow MIDI when asked
(Firefox asks once to add a small "site permission" add-on for this page: that is its MIDI permission).
The mouse and the keyboard play it too, with the Mac app's keys (1-8 and Q-I the pads, space PLAY, A S D F / J K L ;).
Hover over any control to see what it does. HELP (top left) opens the full guide.
